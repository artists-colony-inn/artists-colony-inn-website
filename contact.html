Directions

Getting to Tzfat

By car: Tzfat is approximately 45 minutes from Tiberias, 1.5 hours from Haifa, and 2.5 hours from Tel Aviv via Route 6. Free parking is available near the inn.

By bus: Egged buses connect Tzfat to Haifa, Tiberias, and Jerusalem. The central bus station is a short taxi ride from the Artists' Quarter.

From Ben Gurion Airport: Direct sherut (shared taxi) services run to Tzfat, or take a train to Haifa and connect by bus. We are happy to advise on the best route from your origin.
