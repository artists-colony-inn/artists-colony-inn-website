Policies

House Rules

Meals

  • Breakfast is served daily between 8:00 AM and 10:00 AM.
  • We do not serve a full breakfast on Saturdays and Jewish holidays.
  • On those days, pastries, breads, snacks, and light refreshments are available in the lobby.
  • When Shabbat meals are hosted (Friday night or Saturday), guests are welcome to join us for an additional charge.
  • Shabbat meals, when available, are hosted by our family. We do not operate as a restaurant and do not employ cooks or waitstaff. Guests are welcome to join us at our table and share Shabbat with us.

Check-In and Check-Out

  • Check-in is between 3:00 PM and 7:00 PM.
  • There is no check-in on Shabbat or Jewish holidays.
  • Check-out is by 11:00 AM, except on Shabbat and Jewish holidays, when check-out may be after sunset.
  • We are often flexible with arrival and departure times, but any early or late check-in or check-out must be arranged in advance.
  • Quiet hours are from 10:00 PM to 8:00 AM. Please be considerate of other guests during this time.

Smoking

  • All rooms and indoor common areas are non-smoking.
  • Smoking is permitted outdoors, provided it does not disturb other guests or nearby residents.

Pets

  • Pets are not permitted on the premises.
  • Service animals must be accompanied by appropriate documentation. Animals without documentation are considered pets and are not allowed.

Children

  • We do not accommodate children between the ages of 1 and 10.
  • Exceptions may be made at our discretion. Please contact us before booking.

Conduct

  • Guests are expected to behave respectfully toward other guests, staff, and neighbors.
  • Intoxication, aggressive behavior, excessive noise, or disregard for house rules may result in immediate eviction without refund.

Cancellation Policy

  • More than 7 days before arrival: no penalty.
  • Less than 7 days before arrival: the full cost of the stay will be charged.

Taxes

  • By law, we are required to collect VAT (Value Added Tax) from Israeli citizens and residents.
  • Due to technical limitations, reservations are displayed with VAT included.
  • Guests who are not Israeli residents and present a valid B2 visa together with their passport will not be charged VAT.

Privacy Policy

At the Artists' Colony Inn, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and how we safeguard it when you visit our website or make a reservation with us.

Information We Collect

  • Personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, payment details, and reservation information, when you make a booking or contact us.
  • Technical information including IP address, browser type, device information, and pages visited on our website, collected through cookies and standard analytics tools.
  • Communication preferences any information you choose to share when filling out forms or subscribing to updates.

How We Use Your Information

  • Process and manage reservations and payments.
  • Communicate with you regarding your stay.
  • Send booking confirmations, updates, or messages related to your reservation.
  • Send occasional updates or newsletters, only if you have opted in.
  • Improve our website and overall guest experience.
  • Comply with applicable legal requirements.

Sharing Your Information

We do not sell or trade your personal information. Your data may be shared only with:

  • Trusted service providers who assist us in operating our website and booking systems, under confidentiality obligations.
  • Authorities or parties where disclosure is required by law.

Cookies

Our website uses cookies to ensure proper functionality and to understand how visitors use the site. You may control or disable cookies through your browser settings. Please note that disabling cookies may affect certain site features.

Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse.

Your Rights

  • Request access to the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate or outdated information.
  • Request deletion of your data, subject to legal or contractual requirements.
  • Opt out of marketing communications at any time.
