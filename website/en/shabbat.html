Shabbat at the Artists' Colony Inn

Tzfat is one of the most spiritually alive cities in the world. The Artists' Colony Inn is designed to be a place of rest, quiet, unhurried, and welcoming to guests who observe Shabbat.

Guests who wish to have Shabbat meals at the inn may arrange to do so in one of two ways.

Hosted Shabbat Meal

We invite our guests to join us for a traditionally prepared, strictly kosher Shabbat meal served at the inn. Both Friday night dinner and Shabbat lunch are available at ₪150 per person plus VAT. This service is for inn guests only. Advance booking of at least 3 days is required.

Self-Catered

We provide a space to eat, along with facilities to store and warm food. Please contact us in advance to confirm availability and details.
