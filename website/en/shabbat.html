Shabbat at the Artists' Colony Inn

Tzfat is one of the most spiritually alive cities in the world, and Shabbat is when the city comes most fully into itself. From Friday afternoon the Old City quiets down, the galleries close, and the sound of singing drifts out from the synagogues. It is the best time to be here.

The Artists' Colony Inn is designed around rest and quiet. Guests are welcome to observe Shabbat however they choose -- or simply to enjoy the slower pace without any particular observance. Both are equally at home here.

Check-in and Check-out on Shabbat

We do not check guests in during Shabbat. Guests who are already staying with us are free to leave whenever they wish -- including after sunset, once Shabbat has ended.

Hosted Shabbat Meal

Guests who would like to join us for a Shabbat meal are welcome at our table. The meal is prepared by us, strictly kosher, and served in our dining room. Depending on the weekend, you may be sitting with other guests, with family, or just with us -- the table is always the same, the company changes.

The meal follows our own simple tradition: the blessings, Shalom Aleichem, Eishet Chayil. We keep it unhurried and unpretentious. If guests want to sing together, we are happy to; if not, we eat and talk.

Friday night dinner and Shabbat lunch are each ₪150 per person plus VAT. This is for inn guests only -- we are not able to accommodate outside visitors joining a guest's meal due to space constraints. Advance booking of at least three days is required. We can seat up to fourteen guests at the table.

Self-Catered Shabbat

Guests who prefer to bring or order their own food are fully set up to do so. Every room has a fridge, and we provide a plata -- a Shabbat hot plate -- for keeping food warm. There are additional fridges available if you need more space.

For eating, you have several options: most rooms are comfortable for two people to eat in, the lobby has a larger table that seats four or five, and separate tables in the dining room are also available to self-catering guests -- including during a hosted meal, if you don't mind the company.

Please let us know in advance if you are planning a self-catered Shabbat so we can make sure everything is ready for you.
