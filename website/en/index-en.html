Rooted in Tzfat

Some of these walls are around 350 years old. Stones from Crusader, Mamluk, and Ottoman times sit side by side in the same wall, and somewhere beneath the building, a buried arch hints at rooms still unopened.

This inn was built to be different from the start: not just a hotel, not just a family guesthouse, but rooms gathered around a shared space, giving guests the opportunity to interact if they want to.

This is Tzfat's Artists' Quarter, home to painters and mystics for centuries, still shaping the alleys just outside the door.

Our Meals

We offer our guests a courtesy breakfast and the option of hosted meals for Shabbat and Chagim.

We are Shabbat observant, and kashrut is something we take seriously. Every meal we serve is prepared by us, in our own home, under our own supervision.

As one guest put it, sitting with us in the succah: "This is your home, isn't it? I would eat at your home." That's the trust we hope every guest who sits at our table comes to feel.
