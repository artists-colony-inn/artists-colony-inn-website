Questions worth asking before you book

Tzfat is in the Upper Galilee, well away from the areas that have seen conflict. Life here is calm and normal -- guests arrive, explore the Old City, and leave relaxed. We would not be hosting if we thought otherwise.

The inn itself is in a stone building with walls over a meter thick. The ground floor rooms are considered protected spaces. There is an official public shelter half a block away, though it has not been needed.

We are happy to talk through any concerns before you book.

We set our prices carefully and apply them equally to every guest, regardless of who they are or how they ask. We think that is the fair way to run things.

What we offer every guest is the same: a well-maintained room, a good breakfast, and genuinely personal attention. The value is in the experience, not in how well you negotiate.

We are Shabbat-observant and keep a kosher home. That shapes the inn in a few practical ways: we do not check guests in on Shabbat, and the breakfast we serve is prepared with the best kosher ingredients.

Beyond that, we do not impose anything on our guests. Secular guests, traditional guests, observant guests and non-Jewish guests all stay here comfortably. What we care about is that you have a good stay.

Everything we serve is prepared by us, in our own kitchen, under our personal supervision. We take kashrut seriously and guests who keep kosher rely on what we serve.

If you have specific questions about our standard, ask us before booking and we will give you a straight answer.

The inn is in the heart of the Artists' Quarter, within a short walk of galleries, historic synagogues, the main staircase of the Old City, and the hilltop park. Most of what there is to see in Tzfat is on foot anyway -- the streets are too narrow for cars.

Keep in mind that the Old City is built on a steep hillside. There are stairs everywhere. Comfortable shoes are more important than anything else you pack.

Tzfat's Old City is built on a steep hillside and stairs are unavoidable everywhere. The inn has four approaches -- three to the front entrance and one to the back. Two of the front approaches have many steps; the third has just two small steps and we have successfully helped a wheelchair user arrive this way. Inside, the premises also have steps, and the dining room is on a different level from most rooms.

We will be honest: someone who is fully wheelchair-dependent will not have a comfortable stay with us or in the Old City generally. But if your mobility is partial or you manage stairs with some difficulty, contact us before booking and we will tell you exactly what to expect.

Direct bookings get our best rate. When you book through a third-party platform, part of what you pay goes to them as commission, which does not benefit you in any way. Booking directly with us costs you less and puts us in direct contact from the start.

You may cancel free of charge up to 3 days before your check-in date. After that, the full amount is charged and we are unable to issue refunds.

That said, if something beyond your control prevents you from coming -- an illness, a family emergency, a flight cancellation -- contact us. In most cases we will give you a full credit toward a future stay. It is a case by case decision, but we rarely say no to a reasonable request.

If something comes up, talk to us directly. We are human beings and prefer that to enforcing policies mechanically.
