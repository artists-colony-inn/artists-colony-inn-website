A magical five-star home carved from ancient stone.

Boutique comfort in Tzfat's Old City, in the Artists' Quarter.

Rest, beauty, and the spirit of Tzfat.

Located in Tzfat's Old City Artists' Quarter, the Artists' Colony Inn is just steps from galleries, ancient synagogues, and restaurants. Breathtaking views of Mount Meron and the Galilee open from the terraces and courtyards, a quiet, singular atmosphere carved from ancient Jerusalem stone.

A Building That Remembers

The walls of this building are close to 350 years old, and some of the stones may be older still. A local tour guide and retired archaeologist once pointed out that certain stones carry marks belonging to Crusader, Mamluk, and Ottoman times alike, all present in the same walls. Somewhere beneath the building, a buried arch in the basement hints at rooms still hidden below.
