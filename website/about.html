About the Artists' Colony Inn

The Inn

A boutique inn in the Artists' Quarter of Tzfat's Old City

The Artists' Colony Inn sits within the historic Artists' Quarter — a neighbourhood of ancient stone alleyways, blue-painted doorways, and a creative spirit that has endured for centuries.

The inn occupies a historic building whose walls carry the memory of the city itself. Five individually appointed suites offer guests a genuine home away from home, with the care and attention that only a small, family-run property can provide.

We believe that where you sleep shapes how you experience a place. At the Artists' Colony Inn, every detail is chosen with that in mind.

Your Hosts

Shlomo & Leah

Shlomo and Leah came to Tzfat by choice — drawn by the city's singular character, its deep spiritual roots, and its living tradition of art and learning.

Running the inn is, for them, an expression of genuine hospitality — the kind rooted in the Jewish value of hachnasat orchim, welcoming guests. Every visitor is received as an honoured guest.

From Sunday to Friday, a chef-prepared breakfast is offered to all guests as a courtesy. On Shabbat, a quiet and restful atmosphere is maintained throughout.

The City

Tzfat — a city unlike any other

At 900 metres above sea level in the Galilee, Tzfat is Israel's highest city and one of the four holy cities of Judaism. It has been a centre of Kabbalistic learning since the 16th century.

The old city is compact and walkable — stone staircases, arched passages, and views over the Galilee that stretch to the Golan Heights on a clear day.

Guests often say they arrived for a night and stayed for three.
