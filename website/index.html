The Inn

In the Artists' Quarter of Tzfat's Old City

The mystical alleyways of the Artists' Colony have drawn visitors for centuries. Cool mountain air, golden light, and the quiet that only Tzfat can offer — all just steps from the galleries and the ancient synagogues.

A perfect adult escape: boutique comfort, Shabbat atmosphere, and the spiritual depth of the highest city in Israel.

From Sunday to Friday, a chef-prepared breakfast is offered to our guests as a courtesy.
