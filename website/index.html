Our Story

A home in the heart of Tzfat

The Artists' Colony Inn sits within the historic Artists' Quarter of Tzfat — a city perched high in the Galilee that has drawn mystics, scholars, and artists for generations.

Run by Shlomo and Leah, the inn is a place of genuine hospitality in one of Israel's most remarkable cities. Every guest is welcomed as family.

About the Inn
The City

Why Tzfat?

At 900 metres above sea level, Tzfat is the highest city in Israel — and one of its most ancient. The air is clear, the alleyways are blue, and the atmosphere is unlike anywhere else in the world.
