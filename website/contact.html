Getting Here

Directions

By car: Tzfat is approximately 45 minutes from Tiberias, 1.5 hours from Haifa, and 2.5 hours from Tel Aviv. Free parking is available near the inn.

By bus: Egged buses connect Tzfat to Haifa, Tiberias, and Jerusalem. The central bus station is a short taxi ride from the Artists' Quarter.

From Ben Gurion Airport: Direct sherut services run to Tzfat. We are happy to advise. Just call or WhatsApp us.
